How to play

  • 🎴
    Rip open packs to collect duck cards — from common quacks to legendary shinies.
  • 🗂️
    Build your collection, craft a deck, and trade with friends.
  • ⚔️
    Battle live players, climb Ranked, or take on the Weekly Boss.
  • 🔐
    Sign in once — your collection and progress sync everywhere you play.
⚠️
Flashing lights warning

Getting Ducked contains flashing lights, bright foil/holo effects, and rapid visual flashes during pack rips and battles. This may affect players with photosensitive epilepsy. Please play with care.
